01 — SHOPPING

Finding your favourites

How do I browse the collections?

Visit our Shop or Collections pages to explore different styles, categories and seasonal edits.

How do I find a particular product?

Browse the relevant category, such as Dresses, Tops, Bottoms or Accessories, to discover the pieces available.

Can I ask about a product before purchasing?

Absolutely. Use our Contact page to send a question about any product.

02 — PRODUCTS & SIZING

Details that matter

How do I choose my size?

Check the product's size information and compare it with your measurements. If you are unsure, contact our team for guidance.

How should I care for my clothes?

Always follow the care instructions on the garment label. Different fabrics may need different washing and storage methods.

Will product colours look exactly the same?

Colours can appear slightly different depending on your screen settings and lighting.

03 — ORDERS

Your shopping experience

How can I place an order?

Select a product, review its details and follow the store's checkout instructions. This website is currently a design demonstration and does not process real orders.

Can I change my order?

For a real store order, contact customer support as soon as possible. Changes depend on whether the order has already been processed.

How can I ask about an order?

Visit our Contact page and include your order reference if you have one.

04 — SHIPPING

Getting your pieces to you

Where do you deliver?

Delivery locations depend on the final store's shipping arrangements. Please contact the team for confirmed delivery information.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on the destination, shipping method and order processing. The final store should provide an estimated delivery time at checkout.

Can I track my delivery?

If tracking is available, the store will share the tracking details after dispatch.

05 — RETURNS

A little peace of mind

Can I return an item?

Return eligibility depends on the final store's return policy. Please check the policy before purchasing.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Contact customer support with your order information and clear photographs of the issue.

How long do refunds take?

Refund processing times depend on the store's policy and the payment provider.
